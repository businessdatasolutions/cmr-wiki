# The Big 9 cultural values (Culture 500)

The **Big 9** are the nine cultural values identified by the [[2020-09-01-culture-500-the-research|MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500]] (Sull & Sull / [[CultureX]]) as both the **most frequently cited** in 500+ large firms' official value statements **and** the ones research shows have the **biggest impact on results**. They are the value vocabulary the wiki's culture cluster is built on.

## The nine values (with definitions)

| Value | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Agility** | Employees respond quickly and effectively to marketplace changes and seize new opportunities. |
| **Collaboration** | Employees work well together within their team and across the organization. |
| **Customer** | Employees put customers at the centre — listening and prioritising their needs. |
| **Diversity** | A diverse, inclusive workplace where no one is disadvantaged by gender, race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, religion, or nationality. |
| **Execution** | Employees are empowered to act, have resources, follow process discipline, and are held accountable for results. |
| **Innovation** | The company pioneers novel products, services, technologies, or ways of working. |
| **Integrity** | Employees consistently act honestly and ethically. |
| **Performance** | The company rewards results (compensation, recognition, promotion) and addresses underperformance. |
| **Respect** | Employees show consideration and courtesy and treat each other with dignity. |

(Definitions verbatim from [[2020-09-01-culture-500-the-research]].)

## How the Big 9 were derived

The Culture 500 team analysed firms' official value statements to find the most-cited values, cross-referenced against impact on results, and winnowed to nine (named the "Big 9" in [[2019-06-26-the-9-key-values-for-company-culture]]). Each value is scored per firm via the [[measuring-culture-with-nlp|CultureX NLP pipeline]] as the % of Glassdoor reviews that *positively* mention it (a composite of incidence × sentiment).

## From nine values to three archetypes

[[2024-06-20-oreilly-2024-culture-archetypes-firm-performance|O'Reilly, Cao & Sull (2024)]] factor-analysed the nine into **three [[cultural-archetypes|archetypes]]**:

- **Performance-orientation** — Execution, Agility, Innovation, Performance, Collaboration.
- **People** — Integrity, Respect, Diversity, Collaboration (cross-loads).
- **Customer** — Customer.

See [[oreilly-2024-archetype-factor-loadings]] for the full loading matrix.

## Important distinction: official values ≠ employee-rated drivers

The Big 9 are the values **companies cite**. They are *not* the same as the **[[sull-2021-ten-culture-elements|10 elements that most predict how employees rate culture]]** ([[2021-09-16-ten-things-corporate-culture]], where Respect ranks #1 at ~18×). Several Big-9 values (e.g. Agility, Customer) barely move employees' culture ratings, while drivers like *toxic managers*, *benefits*, and *reorganizations* — not in the Big 9 — matter enormously to employees. The two lists answer different questions: **what firms aspire to** (Big 9) vs **what employees react to** (10 elements).

## Debates and supersession

- The Big 9 are a **proprietary CultureX selection**, not a consensus academic taxonomy. The [[2024-06-20-oreilly-2024-culture-archetypes-firm-performance|archetypes paper]] argues they nonetheless map onto established instruments (Competing Values Framework, Denison DOCS, Hartnell metathemes).
- The nine are derived from **U.S. firms' official value statements (2015–2020)**; cross-cultural and temporal generalisability is open.
- Confidence 0.8: six sources, but all from the same Sull/CultureX programme (non-independent).
