(Bold = the factor each value loads on most strongly. Collaboration cross-loads on Performance and People; Diversity loads on People with a notable negative cross-loading on Customer.)

## How to read it

- **Factor 1 — Performance-orientation:** Execution, Agility, Innovation, Performance, Collaboration. The "get things done / adapt / win" cluster.
- **Factor 2 — People:** Integrity, Respect, Diversity, Collaboration. The "internal coordination / common identity" cluster.
- **Factor 3 — Customer:** Customer (essentially a single-item factor). The "external customer focus" dimension.

The three factors reduce the nine-value vocabulary to a parsimonious structure — see [[cultural-archetypes]] for interpretation and the [[oreilly-2024-archetype-performance-regressions|performance regressions]] for what each archetype predicts.
