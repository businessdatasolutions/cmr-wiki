### Book chapters — strategic risk management (Damodaran)

- [[2008-01-01-damodaran-2008-strategic-risk-taking-ch8-real-options]] — Damodaran (2008) ch 8. The real-options framework: option to delay (patents, natural resources), option to expand (multi-stage projects, growth companies, financial flexibility), option to abandon (escape clauses, switching options). Three preconditions for legitimate use (learning + adaptive behaviour + exclusivity). Worked examples: Avonex patent ($907M), oil reserve ($97M), Disney Mexico ($167M). Confidence 0.85.
- [[2008-01-01-damodaran-2008-strategic-risk-taking-ch11-strategic-risk-management]] — Damodaran (2008) ch 11. The mechanism chapter for SRM: five competitive edges for risk exploitation (information / speed / experience / resources / flexibility) + four pillars of the risk-taking organisation (governance / personnel / reward-punishment / structure-culture). Bowman paradox + 30-year compensation-experiment review. Confidence 0.85.
- [[2008-01-01-damodaran-2008-strategic-risk-taking-ch12-first-principles]] — Damodaran (2008) ch 12. The synthesis chapter: 10 numbered Risk Management principles for strategic risk taking. The natural entry point to the risk-management cluster — distils the rest of the book into memorable rules. Confidence 0.85.

## Concepts

### Strategic management & dynamic capabilities

- [[dynamic-capabilities]] — Teece's framework. A firm's higher-order capacity to create, extend, and modify its resource base via sensing / seizing / transforming clusters. Distinct from operational capabilities. The broader theoretical backbone the W&W process model extends.
- [[warner-wager-process-model]] — **wiki-canonical.** The closed vocabulary of 15 cells across 5 buckets (digital-sensing/* × 3, digital-seizing/* × 3, digital-transforming/* × 3, strategic-renewal/* × 3, contextual/* × 3) used by the `dynamic_capabilities:` frontmatter tag and by the `neighbour-source-scan` skill. Includes the role-defaults matrix mapping cells to 15 business-role slugs.

### Risk management & corporate finance

- [[real-options]] — Real-options analysis applying options-pricing theory to real investments. Three preconditions (learning + adaptive behaviour + exclusivity) and three option types (delay / expand / abandon). The quantitative apparatus for the upside-of-risk reframe.
- [[strategic-risk-management]] — Risk as a strategic variable, not just operational threat. Avoid / hedge / exploit allocation; the upside reframe; cross-functional ownership. Anchored on Damodaran 2008.
- [[two-way-door-decisions]] — Bezos's reversibility heuristic: reversible "two-way door" decisions made fast, irreversible "one-way door" decisions made slow. An instance of real-options reasoning (reversibility = option value).

### Competitive strategy & organisational design

- [[porter-five-forces]] — Porter's framework for industry-structure analysis: five forces (entrants / suppliers / buyers / substitutes / rivalry) determine long-run profitability. The external/industry layer of strategy. Confidence 0.9.
- [[mckinsey-7s-framework]] — Seven interdependent organisational-design elements (Hard Ss + Soft Ss, Shared Values at centre). The internal-alignment layer; complements Porter's external view. Confidence 0.7.

### Corporate innovation

- [[organizational-ambidexterity]] — The explore-vs-exploit capacity: running new-business search and existing-business execution simultaneously, harmonising two antagonistic cultures. The seizing-side `balancing-digital-portfolios` capability in practitioner language. 3 sources.
- [[business-model-innovation]] — Competitive advantage from changing how value is created/delivered/captured, not from product/technology novelty. The Portfolio Map lifecycle + the model-over-technology claim. 2 sources.

### Organizational culture

- [[organizational-culture]] — Values deeply held, widely shared, shaping day-to-day behaviour (Schein's norms-and-values layer). The overarching concept; separates employee-experience drivers from objective-firm-performance drivers. 7 sources.
- [[big-9-cultural-values]] — The nine Culture 500 values (Agility, Collaboration, Customer, Diversity, Execution, Innovation, Integrity, Performance, Respect) with definitions. 6 sources.
- [[cultural-archetypes]] — Performance-orientation / people / customer — the three-factor structure the Big 9 reduce to; only performance-orientation robustly predicts firm financials. 1 source (peer-reviewed).
- [[measuring-culture-with-nlp]] — The CultureX human-in-the-loop NLP method for measuring culture from Glassdoor reviews (20,000+ terms → 80+ values). 6 sources.

### DevOps & DevSecOps

- [[devops]] — Practices + tools + cultural philosophy integrating Dev and Ops; the infinity-loop lifecycle; CI/CD, IaC, microservices; "structure before tools". 4 sources.
- [[devsecops]] — DevOps with security shifted-left into every lifecycle phase as a shared responsibility; SAST/DAST; software-supply-chain; NIST SSDF reference model. 4 sources.
- [[calms-framework]] — Culture / Automation / Lean / Measurement / Sharing — the DevOps adoption-maturity assessment framework (coined by Jez Humble). 1 source.

- [[dora-metrics]] — The four software-delivery metrics: deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, time to restore service. 3 sources.
