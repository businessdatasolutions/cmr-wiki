# Introducing the MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500 — MIT SMR (2019)

> The MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500 brings together MIT expertise and cutting-edge AI to compare and rank the companies leading the U.S. economy using a data set of more than one million Glassdoor reviews. Featuring: The Culture 500 Research Team — Donald Sull and Charlie Sull, cofounders of CultureX.

(MIT SMR's description.)

## TL;DR

A ~100-second launch explainer for the [[2020-09-01-culture-500-the-research|MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500]]. Culture matters (Amazon/Netflix/Trader Joe's turbocharge results; Enron/Miramax show toxic culture destroying companies) — but how do you *measure* it? The Culture 500 analyses **>1 million Glassdoor reviews** and is novel in four ways: (1) rich Glassdoor data; (2) cutting-edge **NLP + human expertise** for accuracy; (3) the **nine cultural values** that companies cite most and that most affect results (the [[big-9-cultural-values|Big 9]]); (4) coverage of firms employing **34 million people** (~¼ of U.S. private-sector employment). Executives can benchmark their culture against competitors and test whether they "walk the talk."
