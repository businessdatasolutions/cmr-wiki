# The 9 Key Values for Company Culture — MIT SMR (2019)

> Through expert analysis and machine learning, the Culture 500 research team determined the 9 key values — the Big 9 — that contribute to a healthy company culture and lead to successful results. Featuring: The Culture 500 Research Team — Donald Sull and Charlie Sull, cofounders of CultureX.

(MIT SMR's description.)

## TL;DR

A ~39-second explainer defining corporate culture as *"a set of values that are deeply held, widely shared, and shape behavior on a day-to-day basis"* and introducing the **[[big-9-cultural-values|Big 9]]** — the nine values that, of all the things companies could value, were **cited most frequently** in firms' official value statements (across 500+ large companies) **and** that research shows have the **biggest impact on results**.

## Results (WHAT)

Two claims: (1) the working **definition of culture** (deeply held + widely shared + behaviour-shaping); (2) the **Big 9** are the intersection of *most-frequently-cited* official values across 500+ firms and *highest-impact-on-results*. The nine: Agility, Collaboration, Customer, Diversity, Execution, Innovation, Integrity, Performance, Respect (enumerated and defined in the [[big-9-cultural-values]] concept).
