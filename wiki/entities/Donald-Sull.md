# Donald Sull

**Donald (Don) Sull** is a senior lecturer at the **MIT Sloan School of Management**, a co-founder of [[CultureX]], and director of MIT's Strategic Agility Project and the **Culture 500**. He is a global expert on strategy and execution in turbulent markets and the connective figure across the wiki's organizational-culture cluster — he co-authored the empirical paper, both MIT SMR articles, and presented the webinars.

## What the wiki cites

- [[2024-06-20-oreilly-2024-culture-archetypes-firm-performance]] — with O'Reilly & Cao (*J. Business Research*, 2024). Culture archetypes → objective firm performance.
- [[2021-09-16-ten-things-corporate-culture]] — with Charles Sull (MIT SMR, 2021). The 10 employee-rated culture elements.
- [[2020-09-01-culture-500-the-research]] — with Charles Sull / CultureX. The Big 9 + Culture 500 method.
- Presenter (with [[Charles-Sull]]) on [[2023-05-31-building-a-winning-workplace-culture]] and the Culture 500 explainer videos ([[2019-06-26-introducing-mit-smr-glassdoor-culture-500]], [[2019-06-26-measuring-company-culture-with-ai]], [[2019-06-26-the-9-key-values-for-company-culture]]).

## Intellectual positioning

Sull's distinctive contribution to the wiki is the **scalable, NLP-based measurement of organizational culture** from employee reviews ([[measuring-culture-with-nlp]]) — the basis for the [[big-9-cultural-values|Big 9]] and the [[cultural-archetypes|three archetypes]]. His earlier work on **strategic agility** and execution in turbulent markets connects to [[dynamic-capabilities]] (the *agility* microfoundation) and [[strategic-risk-management]].
