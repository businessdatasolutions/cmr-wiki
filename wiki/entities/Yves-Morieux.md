# Yves Morieux

**Yves Morieux** is a Managing Director & Senior Partner at **Boston Consulting Group** (Washington D.C. office) and an organisational economist. He is the originator of **[[smart-simplicity|Smart Simplicity]]** and co-author (with Peter Tollman) of *Six Simple Rules: How to Manage Complexity without Getting Complicated* (HBR Press, 2014). His central thesis: organisations respond to rising **complexity** with self-defeating **complicatedness** (layers, silos, process, reporting), and the cure is **cooperation** that better uses people's collective intelligence — not more structure.
