# Strategyzer

**Strategyzer AG** is a Swiss strategy-and-innovation company (headquartered in Préverenges, Switzerland) co-founded by [[Alex-Osterwalder|Alex Osterwalder]] and Yves Pigneur. It commercialises their business-model tools — the **Business Model Canvas**, **Value Proposition Canvas**, and **Business Model Portfolio Map** — through consulting, workshops, training, a software platform, and a substantial freely published **Insights / knowledge library**. The firm's intellectual programme centres on **business-model innovation** and the **explore/exploit** balance set out in *The Invincible Company* (2020).

For the wiki, Strategyzer is the **connective publisher** of the corporate-innovation cluster: three of the wiki's sources are Strategyzer-published material.
