# Alex Osterwalder

**Alex (Alexander) Osterwalder** is a Swiss business theorist and entrepreneur, best known as the **inventor of the Business Model Canvas** — used by more than 200,000 companies across 178 countries — and co-author of *Business Model Generation* (named one of the *Financial Times'* top-10 most-cited business books). He ranks #4 on the Thinkers50 list of global management thinkers and won the Thinkers50 Strategy Award. He is co-founder and CEO of [[Strategyzer]] and has coached senior leaders at Bayer, Bosch, WL Gore, Honeywell, and Mastercard.

For the wiki, his load-bearing contribution is the **business-model-innovation and explore/exploit body of work** (*The Invincible Company*, 2020), which seeds the corporate-innovation cluster.

## Intellectual positioning

Osterwalder's distinctive contribution is the **primacy of the business model**: competitive advantage comes from innovating *how value is created, delivered and captured* rather than from product or technology novelty. His tools (Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas, Business Model Portfolio Map) operationalise this, and *The Invincible Company* frames the **explore/exploit** balance as the defining capability of durable firms.
