# CultureX

**CultureX** is a culture-analytics company co-founded by [[Donald-Sull|Donald Sull]] and [[Charles-Sull|Charles Sull]] (an MIT Sloan–linked venture). It built the **Natural Employee Language Understanding** platform — a human-in-the-loop NLP system that measures organizational culture from employee reviews — and, with MIT Sloan Management Review, produces the **MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500**.

For the wiki, CultureX is the **research organisation behind the entire culture cluster**: its method ([[measuring-culture-with-nlp]]) produces the [[big-9-cultural-values|Big 9]] scores that underpin every culture source here.
