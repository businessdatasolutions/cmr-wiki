# Charles Sull

**Charles (Charlie) Sull** is a co-founder of [[CultureX]] who has advised the senior executive teams of dozens of multinational organizations on cultural management, strategy development, and strategy execution. He co-authors the Culture 500 research with [[Donald-Sull|Donald Sull]].

## What the wiki cites

- [[2021-09-16-ten-things-corporate-culture]] — with Donald Sull (MIT SMR, 2021). The 10 employee-rated culture elements.
- [[2020-09-01-culture-500-the-research]] — with Donald Sull / CultureX. The Big 9 + Culture 500 method.
- Co-presenter on [[2023-05-31-building-a-winning-workplace-culture]] and the Culture 500 explainer videos.
