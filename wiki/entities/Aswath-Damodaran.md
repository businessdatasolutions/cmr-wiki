# Aswath Damodaran

**Aswath Damodaran** is a professor of finance at the NYU Stern School of Business, internationally known for his work on **valuation, corporate finance, and risk management**. He is widely cited as the author of multiple textbook treatments of valuation (the *Investment Valuation* / *Damodaran on Valuation* / *Dark Side of Valuation* trio) and for an extensive freely available teaching corpus published through his NYU Stern faculty page.

For the wiki, his load-bearing contribution is the **2008 book *Strategic Risk Taking: A Framework for Risk Management*** (Wharton School Publishing / Pearson), three chapters of which seed the wiki's risk-management cluster.

## Intellectual positioning

Damodaran's distinctive contribution to the risk-management literature is the **upside reframe**: arguing that risk management is not merely about minimising downside (the dominant traditional-finance treatment) but about *allocating* risk into three buckets — avoid, hedge / pass through, and exploit. He uses standard DCF valuation theory and real-options analysis as the analytical foundations and grounds the framework in named historical cases from US corporate history (GM 1920s, IBM 1950s–60s, Microsoft, Intel, Google, Apple iTunes, J&J Tylenol, Toyota Prius, Southwest Airlines, Booz Allen Knowledge On-Line, eBay-Tom Online).
