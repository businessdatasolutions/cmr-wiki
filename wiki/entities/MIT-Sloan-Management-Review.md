# MIT Sloan Management Review

**MIT Sloan Management Review (MIT SMR)** is the management-research publication affiliated with the MIT Sloan School of Management. It publishes practitioner-facing research articles, videos, and webinars, and — with [[CultureX]] — produces the **MIT SMR/Glassdoor Culture 500**. For the wiki, MIT SMR is the **connective publisher** of the organizational-culture cluster: it is the channel/venue for six of the cluster's seven sources.
