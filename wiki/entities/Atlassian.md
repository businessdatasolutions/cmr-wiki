# Atlassian

**Atlassian** is an Australian enterprise-software company (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Trello, Compass, Open DevOps). For the wiki, Atlassian is the **connective publisher of the DevOps cluster**: its DevOps "topics" and "frameworks" content library supplies four of the cluster's sources, spanning the overview, the culture, and two named frameworks (CALMS, Team Topologies).
